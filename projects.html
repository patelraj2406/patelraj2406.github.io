Modernizing cloud data management for a growing enterprise

Led a 5-person team through a full Microsoft 365 migration — SharePoint Online, Teams, and Azure AD — replacing a fragmented legacy intranet with a governed, AODA-compliant environment. Conestoga College, Cloud Data Management program.

01Project overview

BTC Enterprises — a mid-size pet supply distributor — was running its internal operations on a fragmented, outdated intranet: no standardized folder structure, inconsistent permissions, and no reliable way for remote staff to reach the data they needed. As team captain, I led discovery, planning, and delivery across a 5-person team to replace it with a governed Microsoft 365 environment.

  • Owned end-to-end project leadership: ran weekly stakeholder meetings, assigned workstreams, and kept delivery on schedule across a 6-month timeline.
  • Directed the technical solution architecture — SharePoint Online site structure, Teams collaboration spaces, and Azure AD-based identity and access.
  • Authored the Statement of Work, Content Migration Plan, and Solution Presentation deliverables submitted to the client-facing review board.

02Where the current system was breaking down

The discovery phase surfaced four recurring pain points that were directly costing the business time and increasing risk:

01

Outdated infrastructure

The legacy intranet no longer matched how the organization actually worked or where it was headed.

02

Data disarray

No standardized folder structure or consistent permissions — leading to duplication and real security gaps.

03

Limited accessibility

Remote and mobile access was cumbersome for a workforce that increasingly needed to work flexibly.

04

Fragmented collaboration

Cross-department work was scattered across disconnected storage, causing version-control issues.

Beyond the operational cost, the client also needed the new solution to meet AODA accessibility compliance and Canadian data-retention requirements — which shaped every architecture decision that followed.

03Discovery to deployment

I structured delivery into four phases, each with its own client checkpoint, so the team could validate direction before investing further effort:

  • Discovery — confirmed stakeholders, mapped existing content, and defined success metrics with the client.
  • Design — built the SharePoint site architecture, navigation, and a shared style guide for consistent branding across sections.
  • Migration & development — moved content in two validated passes (Content Migration Plan 1 & 2), configured Azure AD/SSO, and stood up department-specific hubs.
  • Testing & handoff — cross-platform functional testing plus focus groups with end users before final solution presentation and training.

05Outcomes

  • Delivered a governed, permission-based information architecture that eliminated duplicate and orphaned content across six departments.
  • Reduced dependency on IT ticket requests by giving non-technical staff a content management workflow they could run themselves.
  • Brought the client's environment into AODA compliance and aligned with Canadian data-retention requirements.
  • Presented and trained the client's team on the final solution, closing the engagement with a formal solution walkthrough and handoff.

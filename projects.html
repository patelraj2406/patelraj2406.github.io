Watering Plants through the Internet of Things (IoT)

JAN 2017
Soil sensor → Water level check → App notification → Sprinkler auto-start
  • Built an automated IoT watering system for farmers who struggle to water their crops daily.
  • Built a companion application that notifies the user when it's time to turn on the sprinklers.
  • Soil sensors continuously detect moisture level; if it drops below a set threshold, sprinklers start automatically without manual input.
  • Designed to help thousands of farmers and anyone with limited time or living far from their fields.
